# Resources — Voice Projection

## Apps & Tools

| Resource | Type | Why It's Here |
|---|---|---|
| **Pano Tuner** (iOS/Android) | App | Free chromatic tuner, highly accurate, useful for checking resonance pitch |
| **Voice Analyzer** (iOS) | App | Visualizes pitch and volume in real time while you speak |
| **Speeko** | App | Structured public speaking and voice training courses |
| **Vocal Pitch Monitor** (Android) | App | Real-time pitch display, free |

## Key Topics to Search

When looking up technique, these search terms yield the most practical results:
- "diaphragmatic breathing for voice"
- "forward resonance placement speaking"
- "vocal projection without strain"
- "chest voice resonance exercises"
- "consonant articulation exercises for clarity"
