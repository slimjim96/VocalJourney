# Resources — Singing

## Apps & Tools

| Resource | Type | Why It's Here |
|---|---|---|
| **Pano Tuner** (iOS/Android) | App | Real-time chromatic tuner for pitch accuracy checks |
| **Simply Piano / Flowkey** | App | Learn piano basics — helps you play reference notes while doing pitch matching |
| **Solfege / Do Re Mi trainer** | App | Ear training through interval recognition |
| **Smule** | App | Record yourself singing over tracks; forces confrontation with actual pitch |
| **GarageBand** | App (free, Mac/iOS) | Record, play back, and analyze your vocal recordings |

## YouTube Channels

| Channel | Why It's Here |
|---|---|
| **Justin Stoney (New York Vocal Coaching)** | Thorough, science-grounded technique. Covers chest/head voice, vibrato, mix |
| **Brett Manning (Singing Success)** | Extensive library of exercises; good for passaggio and range development |
| **Ramsey Voice Studio** | Accessible, no-fluff breakdowns of common technique problems |
| **Voice Lessons to the World** | Free structured curriculum; goes from beginner to advanced |

## Books

| Title | Author | Why It's Here |
|---|---|
| *The Structure of Singing* | Richard Miller | Definitive technical reference; covers every major technique from the science up |
| *Complete Vocal Technique* | Cathrine Sadolin | Comprehensive; covers all voice registers and effects with clear methodology |
| *Your Singing Voice* | Jeannie Deva | Practical, less academic than Miller; good for self-directed learners |
