# Singing Exercises

Proper singing is controlled breath meeting accurate pitch, sustained with relaxed tone and supported through the phrase. Each exercise below isolates one variable so you can improve them independently before combining.

## Exercises

1. [Pitch Matching](pitch-matching.md) — ear training and landing on notes accurately
2. [Scales and Arpeggios](scales-and-arpeggios.md) — daily warm-up and range development
3. [Breath Control](breath-control.md) — sustaining notes and managing phrase length
4. [Vibrato Foundation](vibrato-foundation.md) — oscillation, onset, jaw release

## Prerequisite

Complete at least one projection breathing exercise before any singing session. The breath mechanics are identical — you're just adding pitch on top.

## Progression

Week 1-2: Pitch Matching + Scales only
Week 3-4: Add Breath Control
Week 5+: Introduce Vibrato Foundation (only after pitch and breath feel stable)
