# Journal

Logging sessions creates a feedback loop that practice alone can't provide. Over time, patterns emerge — what you keep struggling with, what clicked, how your stamina changes — and that information guides what to do next.

## How to Use

1. Copy `template.md` into `logs/` and rename it to today's date: `YYYY-MM-DD.md`
2. Fill it out immediately after your session while it's fresh
3. Be honest — vague entries help no one

## Prompts to Think Through

- What felt different than last time?
- What would you change about the session?
- What's one thing that genuinely improved?
- What do you want to focus on next time?

## Log Files

Each log is named by date and lives in [`logs/`](logs/). They accumulate automatically — no reorganization needed.
